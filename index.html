01 / OVERVIEW

From scattered enquiries to a clearer pipeline.

This project demonstrates how a small sales team could use a structured lead tracker and a dashboard to keep enquiry details, lead status, follow-up dates, and opportunity values organised. The workbook includes a dashboard, lead tracker, and status summary.

Data noteAll lead records and metrics are fictional sample data for demonstration. They are not real customer information or actual business results.

02 / KEY FEATURES

Designed to make follow-ups visible.

01

Lead tracker

Organised lead details, source, status, owner, estimated value, and next follow-up date.

02

Performance dashboard

At-a-glance indicators for lead volume, qualification, overdue follow-ups, and pipeline value.

03

Status summary

A breakdown of leads across pipeline stages to make progress easier to review.

04

Follow-up visibility

Dates and statuses help users spot leads that may need timely attention.

03 / WORKFLOW

A simple, repeatable sales workflow.

1

Capture
Record a new lead and enquiry details.

2

Qualify
Update the lead's stage and opportunity value.

3

Follow up
Set the next action date and keep records updated.

4

Review
Use dashboard summaries to review pipeline progress.
