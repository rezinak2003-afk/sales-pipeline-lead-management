Business problem and approach

A practical, interview-discussable workflow rather than a decorative dashboard.

The problem

What this addresses

Sales teams can lose opportunities when lead qualification notes, next steps and follow-up dates are scattered across messages or individual sheets.

The build

What is included

This workbook centralises lead details, current stage, estimated value, ownership and next follow-up date, then summarises the pipeline in a dashboard.

Data integrity

How to interpret it

Sample records are synthetic. Formulas support the workflow, and the workbook includes a usage guide and documented limitations.
