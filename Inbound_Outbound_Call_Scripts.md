# Inbound / Outbound Call Scripts

These are practice scripts. Replace bracketed fields with a truthful, approved product description before using them with real prospects.

## Inbound enquiry

**Opening:**  
“Thanks for contacting [Company]. My name is [Name]. To make this useful, may I ask what prompted your enquiry today?”

**Discovery:**  
“What are you using today to manage [process]?”  
“What is the biggest issue or inefficiency you would like to improve?”  
“What would a better process look like for your team?”  
“Is there a timeframe you are working toward?”

**Value connection:**  
“Based on what you shared, [specific capability] may help with [need the prospect named]. I can show you how it works and explain any limitations.”

**Close:**  
“Would [time option A] or [time option B] be better for a short discussion?”

## Outbound prospecting

**Permission-based opening:**  
“Hello [Name], I’m [Name] from [Company]. We work with [type of organisation] on [specific process]. Is now a bad time for a 30-second explanation?”

**Reason for call:**  
“I’m reaching out because [public, relevant business context]. I wanted to learn how your team handles [process] and see whether a short conversation would be relevant.”

**Qualification:**  
“How is this managed today?”  
“Who else is involved in evaluating a change?”  
“What would make a solution worth considering?”

**Respectful close:**  
“If this is not relevant, I’m happy to close the loop. If it is, may I send a concise overview and follow up at a time you choose?”

## Follow-up email

**Subject:** Follow-up on [business need]

Hello [Name],

Thank you for speaking with me. I noted that your team is looking at [need] and that [priority/timing] matters. As discussed, here is [approved information or resource].

Would [date/time] work for a brief next-step conversation? If priorities have changed, please let me know and I will update my follow-up accordingly.

Regards,  
[Name]  
[Company]

## Call notes to record

- Prospect need and current process
- Fit / qualification outcome and reason
- Questions or objections raised
- Any information promised
- Agreed next step, owner and date
- Do-not-contact or preferred-channel requests

Never invent product results, guarantee commercial outcomes, or continue contacting someone who has asked not to be contacted.
